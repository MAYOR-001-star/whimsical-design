For game makers

Got a game? Put it on start.ng

Four small steps from your laptop to yourgame.start.ng. Make games anywhere on the continent? Yours goes up for free.

1

Build it

Any static browser game: HTML, JavaScript, three.js, Phaser. Players bring their start.ng character, scores and friends.

2

Push it to GitHub

A public repo with a ready-to-play index.html at the top or in dist/, plus a cover.png for its card.

3

Publish it

One API call with your repo link, no key needed. Or hand your AI start.ng/docs and let it do the lot.

4

Approve it once

Open the link you get back and it's live at yourgame.start.ng, with its own leaderboard if you want one. After that, every push updates it.

About start.ng

Nigeria's home for browser games.

Built by the HNG community. Every game is free to play in your browser with no download, and many are made in Naija: danfo rush hour, jollof catching, Lagos markets and more.

We're also building a home for web games from across Africa.

More about start.ng

Questions, answered

Is it free?

Yes. Every game on start.ng is free to play in your browser, with nothing to download.

Do I need an account to play?

No. Sign in to get your start.ng character, keep your scores on the leaderboards and join the daily tournament.

Are there Nigerian or African web games?

Yes. Many games here are Naija-themed, from danfo rush hour to jollof and Lagos markets, and we welcome games from across Africa.
